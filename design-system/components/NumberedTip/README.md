# NumberedTip

A big rose numeral beside a suggestion, on a soft white blob: the source deck's *Algunas sugerencias* layout.

- Props: `n`, children (the tip, one or two sentences), `done` and `onToggle` (adds the "Add to my routine" switch).
- Adopted tips turn their numeral `ochre` and the switch reads "✓ In my routine".
- Lay tips out two per row on desktop, one per row under 720px.

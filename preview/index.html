App flow

The finished screens, linked the way the app works. Start at Login and move with the menu and the buttons on each page. The AI helper is in the menu and also one tap away from the tool it reads, marked with a sparkle. Habits you create, sheets you start, targets you save, tasks you cross out and what the helper can see stay until you reset the demo or close the tab.

AI helper

Menu · AI helper has two things: one chat and one weekly report. The rest of the helper lives inside the tools, marked with a sparkle. Every screen reads the same demo data as the tools, and nothing changes until you press a button.

Ask the helper new
helper.html · was Plan with AI + Ask your data
  • Where do I start? → next step from Home's tasks, by energy
  • Break down a task → steps in the list; I'll do it now → Done crosses it out on Home too
  • Which habits were hardest this month? → Show me by week
  • Manage what the helper can see → turn a source off and ask again
